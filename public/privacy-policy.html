Welcome to Nourin TicTacToe, developed by Uddin Traders. This game is designed as a fun, child-friendly space-themed Tic-Tac-Toe experience. Our primary goal is to provide a safe environment for everyone, especially children.

1. Children's Privacy (Under 13)

This app is intended for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines. We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal information from children.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Nourin TicTacToe does NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your device's contacts or camera.
  • We do not use any third-party data tracking or behavioral advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app does not use any third-party tools or analytics services that collect your data. Everything in the game is processed locally on your device for game-play purposes only.

4. Ads and Purchases

This version of the application does not contain advertisements or in-app purchases, ensuring a clean and safe interface for children to play without distractions.

5. Security

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no risk of data leakage. However, we ensure the integrity of the application code to provide a secure offline experience.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
